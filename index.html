in_short

A prey species can only be identified if the reference database can return it. Classifiers such as QIIME 2's naive Bayes are trained on references cut to the amplified fragment, so a species can be in the database yet impossible to return. Its reads go to a surviving relative or higher rank, and nothing in the output separates this from the prey not being eaten.

Barcode-compliant is not Leray-compliant. jgHCO2198 is a degenerate form of the Folmer reverse primer, so it binds exactly where the 658 bp barcode ends. A barcode deposited a few bases short at its 3′ end keeps almost the whole region but loses the site the primer needs.

data_and_methods

In silico PCR with CRABS v1.14.0 at up to 4 primer mismatches, amplicons filtered to 290–340 bp, against MIDORI2 GenBank release 269 (UNIQ). Database naming figures: BOLD and NCBI downloads of May 2026, species counted only where the name is a full binomial. BIN figures: BOLD_Public.02-Oct-2026 (DOI 10.5883/DP-BOLD_Public.02-Oct-2026). Prey genera: working list for Los Amigos, Peru, anchored to Larson et al. (2022). The Gryllus assimilis example uses QIIME 2 extract-reads at identity 0.7. This is ongoing work; figures are being re-run on dated snapshots (MIDORI2 GB274, BOLD 02-Oct-2026, NCBI November 2026).
